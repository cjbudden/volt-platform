Playback reports now include Delivery, from the CDN's own logs of every request: errors, cache hits, time to first byte at the edge and at the origin, segment delivery speed and requests made with an empty buffer. Viewers whose player sends no reports, such as a stream address opened in another player, are now counted, and Telemetry health shows how many sessions report themselves.
