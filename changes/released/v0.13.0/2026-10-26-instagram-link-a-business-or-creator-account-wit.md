Instagram: link a Business or Creator account with Instagram Login or through the Facebook Page it is tied to, then publish clips as Reels and Image Library pictures as feed posts. Before you publish, each Instagram account shows how many of its posts for the last 24 hours are used; captions are checked against Instagram's 2,200 characters and 30 hashtags, and views, likes and follower counts are read back.
