Assets can now carry more than one image. Video keeps its Thumbnail and audio its Cover as the main image, and each asset also offers a Poster, Hero backdrop, Banner, Social share card and Title art for portals, apps and feeds, plus any image types your account adds (Image types… in the asset's image panel). A type without its own image is filled from the main image, cropped to the right shape, so a portal always gets a picture that fits; you can set the focus point that crops keep in frame.
