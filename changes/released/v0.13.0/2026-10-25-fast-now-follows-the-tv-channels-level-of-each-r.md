FAST now follows the TV channels level of each role: View to see FAST channels, run orders and previews, Operate to put a channel on or off air and activate a run order, Manage to create channels and edit run orders, ads and ad servers. A role without TV channels can no longer read or change FAST channels through the API. Ad breaks follow their stream: an event's breaks need Events and a station's need Radio stations.
