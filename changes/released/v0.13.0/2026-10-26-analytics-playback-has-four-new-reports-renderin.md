Analytics > Playback has four new reports: Rendering and devices (dropped frames, decoder errors, hardware decoding, DRM system), Access and DRM (why the play gate said no and how long it took, licence time and failures, tokens refused part way through), Live streams (how far behind live viewers are and how much that drifts, by device) and Advertising (breaks watched to the end, ad startup and buffering, time back to the programme, viewers who left during an ad).
