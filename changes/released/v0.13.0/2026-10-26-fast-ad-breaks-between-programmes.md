FAST channels can schedule ad breaks between programmes only, with break lengths earned by the content before them, breaks that wait until enough short titles have played, different ad rules by time of day, a cap on ad minutes per clock hour, grid pad sold as ad time, ad-free or "only after" rules per title or slot, and limits on ads per break with one ad per advertiser.
