The Volt player SDK has VoltJourney: a small, fixed set of app events (app open, screens, the title picked and where from, search, paywall and purchase, play) that ties how a viewer reached the play button to what happened after it. Nothing is sent until the viewer's consent allows it, search words are never sent, and the same events can be passed on to Google Analytics 4.
