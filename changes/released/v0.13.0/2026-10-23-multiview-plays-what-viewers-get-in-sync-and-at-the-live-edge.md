Multiview now plays the output viewers are actually given, at the live edge and with sound in step with the picture. Starting and stopping a tile no longer jumps back several minutes. Each tile shows source and output flags (NO SOURCE, failover, filler, dropped outputs, timing), and the wall pages itself to what fits your screen. "Viewer location" plays every tile as a viewer in a chosen country sees it, with the rights decision and its reason beside the picture.
