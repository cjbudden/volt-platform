FAST channels can use **Loops & Fillers** for filler: under Build > Brand & filler, pick the **slate** (plays in ad breaks nobody sold, and in gaps when there is no loop) and the **gap loop** (fills the gaps between programmes in the slate's place, with or without idents), and optionally play the loop in unsold breaks too. Each slate or loop is rendered once into a broadcast-shaped clip (1920×1080, 25 fps, stereo; a still slate becomes 10 s with silence, a loop up to 2 min, given silence if it has none), prepared for air like any FAST slate, and shared by every channel that uses it. These clips stay out of the Media Library.
