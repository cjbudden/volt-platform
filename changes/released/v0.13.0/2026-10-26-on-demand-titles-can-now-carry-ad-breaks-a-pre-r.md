On-demand titles can now carry ad breaks: a pre-roll, mid-rolls at a title's own cue points or every few minutes, and a post-roll, filled by your ad server and then your house spots. Set it up under Admin > On-demand ads for the account or per content type. Viewers who paid play without ads, Apple devices fetch the ads themselves, and every other player gets them stitched in.
