Analytics > Playback has two new reports for apps that send their journey events: Journey to play (where viewers give up between opening the app and the first frame, including the paywall, and how long each step takes) and Where plays come from (plays, watch time, completion and playback quality by row, search, deep link, push notification and more, and titles whose page was opened but never played). Every playback report can now be filtered by where the viewer came from and by how many screens they saw before pressing play.
