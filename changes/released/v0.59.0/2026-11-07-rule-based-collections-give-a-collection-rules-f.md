Rule-based collections. Give a collection rules (for example: genre is Sport and first aired in the last two weeks) and it keeps itself up to date: new and changed media joins or leaves within seconds, and every rule collection is checked in full each night. Rules test title, genre, tags, length, content type, rights profile, provider, dates and your content types' own fields, with all or any and groups, and show how many titles match as you write them.
