FAST channel idents can now play after every few programmes instead of after every one: on the channel's Brand tab, choose Idents between programmes and set "Play an ident after every N programmes". The titles in between run back to back as a group and the ident closes it, which suits short titles. The ident lands in the same place on every re-plan, and the count starts again at the daily restart. Apply the lineup after changing it so the guide makes room.
