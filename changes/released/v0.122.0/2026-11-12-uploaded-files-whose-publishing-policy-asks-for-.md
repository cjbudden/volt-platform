Uploaded files whose publishing policy asks for DRM are now encrypted when they are packaged (Widevine, PlayReady and FairPlay, with a key from your account's DRM key service) and get no MP4 download. The Encode tab names the DRM systems, and the upload band says before you add files whether they will be packaged with DRM.
