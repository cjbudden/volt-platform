A collection can now be sold as a package, or limited by app and region: choose the apps and countries it applies to and, region by region, whether its titles are free, need viewers to sign in, or need a package bought, priced per app. The strictest collection a title is in wins in each app and country, buying any of the packages that lock it unlocks it, a sub-collection can only tighten its parent's access, and a title whose rights rule out the collection's access is refused with the reason. Set through the API for now, with View as to check what a viewer gets.
