FAST channels apply each title's rights where viewers are: while a channel airs, a viewer outside a title's territories, FAST rights or rights window (or the geo of its publishing plan) sees the channel's geo replacement for that programme, then the channel again. Choose the replacement, a loop or slate from Loops & Fillers, under Build > Brand & filler, where the programmes restricted in the next two days are listed. Settings has a new Where it can be watched for the whole channel. The FAST guide is rewritten to cover every tab and option, with examples of what each does on air.
