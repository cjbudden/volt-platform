Metadata rules: give a policy to every title whose metadata, tags, collections or source meet a set of conditions, for example every title tagged premier-league. The first rule a title meets applies, so you set the order by dragging; it wins over the content type and the channel, event or station the title came from, and Test a title shows which rule a title meets and what it then gets. Published titles take up a change within a minute.
