Analytics has a new Playback view: what viewers actually saw, from the Volt player's own reports. Playback success (attempts, startup failures with the step and code, exits before the first frame, failures after the start), startup time at p50, p95 and p99 with its steps, buffering, quality and bitrate switching, engagement and the health of the reports themselves, all filtered by content, live or on demand, device, OS, browser, app and SDK version, CDN, country, region and network. A Worst segments table ranks any of these by any measure, so a bad device or app release stands out.
