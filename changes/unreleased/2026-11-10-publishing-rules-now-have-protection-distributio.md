Publishing rules now have protection, distribution and retention sections (DRM, WAF profile, token lifetime, MP4 download, share links, retention and more), each resolved field by field. A rule can lock fields so the rules beneath it cannot change them, start from one of five presets (Open, Members, Geo-locked, Premium, Rights-managed), and is checked for conflicts before it is saved, with a dry run that shows how many titles it governs and changes. Available through the API; the console screens follow.
