Single sign-on for admin users: connect Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, Google Workspace or any OpenID Connect or SAML 2.0 provider in Admin > Single sign-on, verify your email domain with a DNS record, and your team signs in with "Continue with …". New people can be created on their first sign-in with roles from their provider groups, the provider's two-step sign-in can count as Volt's, and SSO can be made compulsory (account owners keep a password as the way back in).
