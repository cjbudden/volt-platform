Each QC finding on a slate or loop now shows stills from the preview at that moment (for a jump where a loop restarts, its last frame beside its first), plays that moment with one click, and can be accepted on its own, with a note. Accepted findings stay accepted when QC runs again, and once every error is accepted the item can be approved without a note.
