Your ad server can now decide where the breaks go in on-demand titles (VMAP, as Google Ad Manager's ad rules and FreeWheel do): choose The ad server places them (VMAP) under Where the breaks go. Volt asks for each viewer's schedule, puts each break on the nearest point where the video can switch, plays each break's ads from the ad server, and falls back to your own break settings with house spots when the ad server is slow or down.
