Every new slate and loop gets a house preview: it is built by the same encoder and packager your streams use, in your house format, and plays continuously in the console so you can see whether its join is seamless. QC checks the preview (black, silence, loudness, the cut to whole segments, a jump or click where the loop restarts, upscaling, bars, frame rate and interlacing), and each finding jumps to its place in the loop.
