Images Volt saves for an asset are now named after the asset, like sintel-1080p-21fe57ffc0.jpg, in a folder named the same way and dated by the programme, the recording or the day it was added (no more 1970-01-01, and no .mkv in folder names). Existing images are renamed in the background, everything that uses them keeps working, and renaming an asset renames its images.
