Hybrid Sites add-on: Admin, Sites lists your own locations running the Volt site agent, with each site's health, its servers (cores, memory, graphics cards and their encode sessions, free disk, versions, last contact) and a one-time enrolment token to add a server, which then waits for Approve or Reject. Offered only to accounts with the add-on.
