The Media Library has a grid view beside the list, with keyscreens, content type, availability, how complete each asset's metadata is and its top rendition. Filter by content type, availability, source, missing required metadata, resolution, codec, date and duration, and share the filtered view by its link. Each asset shows the metadata its type needs (an episode's series, season and episode number, a match's teams) and can fill it from the programme guide, never replacing what you typed. A new Encode tab shows the renditions, audio, captions, packaging and DRM.
