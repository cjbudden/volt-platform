Media > Encoding Queue shows what is being encoded now, what is waiting and in which order, and what finished recently. Open a job to see each output as it is made (every rendition, HLS and DASH, the thumbnails and the sprite sheets), the encoder's log while it runs, and each attempt. Failed encodes can be retried from there, and a thumbnail or sprite step that fails is now reported rather than skipped silently.
