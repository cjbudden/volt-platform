Social formats: Volt follows the faces in a clip and frames a 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5 version of it. The new Formats tab on every asset shows the crop over the picture, a timeline of shots and keyframes and live previews of each format; drag the crop to correct it, choose who a shot follows, approve and render. Shots where two people are too far apart show the whole picture over a blurred fill and are flagged for a look.
