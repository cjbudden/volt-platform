area: Trick-play
---
Live channels and events now get I-frame playlists in their HLS when their output profile has trick-play on, from their next start, so Apple devices and smart TVs show pictures while scrubbing back through the live window. Nothing is re-encoded. Viewers in a rights blackout get the stream without trick-play, never pictures of the programme they may not see.
