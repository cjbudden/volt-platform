Every Volt player now has a Playback stats panel: right-click the picture (or press Shift+D) to see the resolution, dropped frames, codecs, bitrate, connection speed, buffer, live latency and the CDN edge that served the stream, and copy it all for a support ticket. In the console it is on the channel output monitor (a Stats button beside HLS / DASH), the source and output previews, and every recording and media preview.
