Blackout and replacement windows from the guide can now differ by audience at the same moment: a viewer in Australia can be replaced from 20:00 to 21:00 while one in New Zealand is replaced from 21:00 to 22:00, each decided per viewer at the CDN on the same play address. Programmes can also exempt a rights class (for example a premium tier) from a window. On a single server the public live address makes the same decision, and outside production you can test it with ?as=AU, &rc= and &at= to see what a viewer elsewhere, or at another time, would get.
