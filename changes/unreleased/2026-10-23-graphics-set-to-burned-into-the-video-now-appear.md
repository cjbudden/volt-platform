Graphics set to Burned into the video now appear in the picture itself on transcode channels and events, so every viewer, recording, clip and third-party player sees them. Take them on, change them and take them off in live control as usual; they reach the encoded picture in well under a second, and leave it during ad breaks that hide graphics. A graphic assigned to burn while the stream is running appears from its next start.
