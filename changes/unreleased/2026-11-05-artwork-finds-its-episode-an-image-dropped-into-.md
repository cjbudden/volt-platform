Artwork finds its episode: an image dropped into a watchfolder with a type word at the end of its name, such as Scrubs-s1-e1_poster.png or Scrubs-s1-e1-thumbnail.jpg, becomes that video's Poster or Thumbnail, as if chosen on the asset page. A rule can also give every image it takes an image type.
