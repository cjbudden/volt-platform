FAST channels can carry their own brand: idents that play between programmes (in the gaps, or between every programme like a broadcaster) and a brand loop that fills the rest of a gap. The On air tab now shows what is going to air next, to the second, under the preview: programmes, ad breaks, idents and the brand loop, with a countdown to the next item.
