Publishing Rules are laid out as the five sections of a policy (Lifecycle, Access, Protection, Distribution and Retention), with DRM, playback token lifetime, WAF profile, referrer domains, concurrent streams, MP4 download, share links, FAST, sitemap and retention fields. Each field has a padlock: a locked field cannot be changed by the content types, streams or items beneath it, and they show who locked it. Start a rule from a preset (Open, Members, Geo-locked, Premium, Rights-managed).
