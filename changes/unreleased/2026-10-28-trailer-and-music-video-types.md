**Trailer and Music video content types.** Two new standard content types for on-demand assets. **Trailer / promo** (schema.org VideoObject): title, what it is a trailer for, kind (trailer, teaser, promo, featurette, clip), description, release date, genre, rating, language and image. **Music video** (schema.org MusicVideoObject): title, artist, album, director, genre, label, ISRC, release date, explicit and image, matching the Song type so a music channel reads both the same way. Every account gets them automatically, and the library's metadata completeness covers them.
