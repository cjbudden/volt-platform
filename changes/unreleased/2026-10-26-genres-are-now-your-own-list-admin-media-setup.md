Genres are now your own list. In Admin > Genres you can add, rename, reorder, retire and merge the genres your stations, channels, events and on-demand assets are tagged with; every Genre and Category field offers your list. Renaming or merging a genre updates everything already tagged with it, a retired genre stays on what has it but is no longer offered, and a genre in use cannot be removed by accident.
