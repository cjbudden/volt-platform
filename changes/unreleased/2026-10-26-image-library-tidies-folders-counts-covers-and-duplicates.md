The Image Library tidies itself. Folders that retention or an asset's removal leaves empty are removed, and folder counts now match what the grid shows. Song artwork has its own Cover type and filter. Duplicates… finds images stored more than once, keeps one and points everything that used a copy at it. Choosing an image for an asset now opens on that asset's own frames, uploads and stills first, and a video is no longer offered song covers.
