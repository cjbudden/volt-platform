Copy rules can now mirror a storage target: when an item is deleted from Volt and leaves the restore bin, its copies at that target are deleted too, after a waiting period you set per target (7 days by default, 0 to 90). Volt never deletes a file someone changed at your site, recordings are always kept, a restore cancels anything waiting, and every delete is in the audit trail.
