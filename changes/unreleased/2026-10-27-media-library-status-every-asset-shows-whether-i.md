Media Library status: every asset shows whether it can be used (Ready, Not checked, QC failed, Building, Build failed, Will not play, No media), with a Status filter and a Problems shortcut. A failed build says why on the asset and offers Build again, or Build accepting the gaps when the recording has holes. Only Ready assets are offered to FAST channels, planned onto air, or accepted as a blackout replacement.
