Where a graphic renders

    
One template, three renderers. A binding picks any combination.

  

  

    

      
Free In the player

      
Drawn over the video in the browser, so it can differ by viewer: a sponsor in one
      territory, another elsewhere. Reports its own impressions.

      
Cost nothing. Does not survive download or redistribution, and an
      ad blocker can hide it.

    

    

      
Free In the operator preview

      
The same code path as the player, so what the operator approves is what the viewer gets.
      Never a separate implementation.

      
Cost nothing. This is the reason the template language is constrained.

    

    

      
Metered Burned into the picture

      
Composited into the encode, so it survives the recording, the clip, the download and any
      redistribution. The right answer for a contracted sponsor mark.

      
Cost one blend per frame, because it happens once before the ladder
      splits. Identical for every viewer, so never use it for targeted inventory.

That last one matters. Render a card whose parameters do not match the asset
    and ffmpeg joins it anyway, exits cleanly, and produces a file that reports the wrong duration
    and the wrong resolution. The publish step verifies the packet count against the sum of the
    parts before anything reaches a viewer.

  






  

    
What we want your view on

    
These four answers change what gets built, and in what order.

  

  

    
Who drives the graphic on the day?
      
A person in the gallery with this panel open, or a data feed from the scoring system with
      nobody watching it? Both are supported. The first one shapes the console.

    
Who makes the templates?
      
A set we design and you restyle, or your own designers uploading their own HTML? This is
      the single biggest difference in scope.

    
Must sponsor graphics appear in clips and downloads?
      
If yes, they have to be burned in live rather than added at publish time, which brings the
      metered path forward.

    
Is a banner here advertising, or branding?
      
Branding should be burned in. Advertising should not, because a burned banner is identical
      for all ten thousand viewers and sells for a fraction of a targeted one.
