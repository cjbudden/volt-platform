Changes to Workflows.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **Workflows:** Work done at your own sites now shows on the Workflows map and in its report, in a new Your sites lane: Encode at site for Hybrid Sites, and Archive copies for Mirror and Archive, each with its runs, error rate, times, retries and most common error. Each add-on brings only its own stage.

## Fixed

- **Workflows:** Copies to your own storage were left out of the Workflows report altogether, and encodes at a site were listed only under Other background work; both now count on their stage, including days already reported.
