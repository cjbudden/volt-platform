Changes to Billing.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **Billing:** Where your invoices are charged to the card on file, each monthly invoice is now charged automatically; a failed payment is tried again and your owners and administrators are told, without your service being interrupted. Every invoice can also be downloaded as a PDF (GET /api/billing/invoices/\{id\}/pdf).
