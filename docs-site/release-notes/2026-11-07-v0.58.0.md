Changes to Account and VOD.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **Account:** Mirror and Archive is now in the console: the Storage page lists your storage targets at your sites and every copy with its state, why a copy is waiting and what to do, and you can add or change a target and check a copy again with one click.
- **VOD:** Publishing rules have an Archive copies field for accounts with Mirror and Archive: choose which storage targets an original is copied to, when it lands or when it is published, and an optional off-peak window.

## Improved

- **Account:** Sites is in the menu for accounts with Mirror and Archive alone, and Volt can switch the add-on on for an account in System Admin.
