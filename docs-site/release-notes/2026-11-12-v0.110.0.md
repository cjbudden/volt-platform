Changes to Billing and Media Library.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **Billing:** AI Find details is a new add-on: 500 lookups a month for $15, with packs of 1,000, 5,000 and 20,000 more. When the month's lookups are used up, nothing more is sent to the AI model until a bigger pack is chosen or the month starts again.
- **Media Library:** Find details can read a page with an AI model: with the new AI Find details add-on on, Read with AI suggests values for a page that publishes no details for search engines, each with the sentence of the page it came from, for you to check and apply.
