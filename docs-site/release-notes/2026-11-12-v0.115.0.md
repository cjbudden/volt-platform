Changes to Play gate and Publishing Rules.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **Play gate:** The play gate counts a signed-in viewer's plays where a Concurrent streams limit applies: the answer carries streams and sid, the viewer report of a stopped play answers stop with code concurrent_streams and the words to show, and a stopped play asking again gets 409.
- **Publishing Rules:** A publishing rule's Concurrent streams limit now applies to members-only and premium titles and channels: when a signed-in viewer starts more plays than it allows, their oldest play is stopped and the player is told why. Free content is never limited.
