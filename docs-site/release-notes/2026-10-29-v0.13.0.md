## New

- **Access:** **FAST channels have their own permission, and a FAST Manager role.** Building and running FAST channels is now its own area in roles ("FAST channels": view, operate, manage), separate from live TV channels, and covers a FAST channel's own pages too. Encoding profiles and ladders are their own area as well, out of Account settings, and handling the ingest queue (held uploads, metadata, duplicates) is part of Files, uploads & storage. Existing roles keep exactly the access they had. The new built-in **FAST Manager** role runs FAST channels, the titles they air, the Image Library, Loops & Fillers, uploads and the encode queue, and ladders, and sees reports, with no access to live TV, radio, events, users or account settings. `npm run seed:fast-user` adds a FAST test user to the local demo account.
- **Account:** Admin > Formats & reframing: choose the formats Volt makes, what to keep in frame, how the framing may move, whether a person approves it, and which format each social platform receives. Sending each platform its own format is off until you turn it on.
- **Account:** In the Image Library you can now select images (tick them, or Shift-click a range) and move them to another folder, or drag them onto a folder in the tree. Everything that uses a moved image keeps working, including assets, channel slates and logos, metadata and links already shared. An empty folder can be deleted from its folder view; a folder that still holds images says how many and where.
- **Account:** Single sign-on for admin users: connect Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, Google Workspace or any OpenID Connect or SAML 2.0 provider in Admin > Single sign-on, verify your email domain with a DNS record, and your team signs in with "Continue with …". New people can be created on their first sign-in with roles from their provider groups, the provider's two-step sign-in can count as Volt's, and SSO can be made compulsory (account owners keep a password as the way back in).
- **Account:** In the Image Library you can replace an image with another (a duplicate, a better version, or a broken upload): everything that used it switches to the one you keep, and the old one goes to the restore bin. Duplicates show where each copy is used and can be merged one group at a time, and Delete now lists every place an image is used.
- **Ads:** Ad clicks are tracked. When a viewer clicks Learn more on an on-demand ad, or a player opens a FAST ad's click address, Volt records the click, tells the ad server and opens the advertiser's page. Clicks show in the on-demand ad report.
- **Analytics:** Analytics has a new Playback view: what viewers actually saw, from the Volt player's own reports. Playback success (attempts, startup failures with the step and code, exits before the first frame, failures after the start), startup time at p50, p95 and p99 with its steps, buffering, quality and bitrate switching, engagement and the health of the reports themselves, all filtered by content, live or on demand, device, OS, browser, app and SDK version, CDN, country, region and network. A Worst segments table ranks any of these by any measure, so a bad device or app release stands out.
- **Analytics:** Analytics > Playback has four new reports: Rendering and devices (dropped frames, decoder errors, hardware decoding, DRM system), Access and DRM (why the play gate said no and how long it took, licence time and failures, tokens refused part way through), Live streams (how far behind live viewers are and how much that drifts, by device) and Advertising (breaks watched to the end, ad startup and buffering, time back to the programme, viewers who left during an ad).
- **Analytics:** Analytics > Playback has two new reports for apps that send their journey events: Journey to play (where viewers give up between opening the app and the first frame, including the paywall, and how long each step takes) and Where plays come from (plays, watch time, completion and playback quality by row, search, deep link, push notification and more, and titles whose page was opened but never played). Every playback report can now be filtered by where the viewer came from and by how many screens they saw before pressing play.
- **Analytics:** Playback reports show how many ads were played and how many were watched to the end, and each session lists its ad count.
- **Analytics:** Under Sessions in Analytics > Playback you can open any viewer's session and see everything that happened to it in order: what they saw second by second, every report their player sent, the play gate's answer and the ad beacons sent for them.
- **Analytics:** Playback reports now include Delivery, from the CDN's own logs of every request: errors, cache hits, time to first byte at the edge and at the origin, segment delivery speed and requests made with an empty buffer. Viewers whose player sends no reports, such as a stream address opened in another player, are now counted, and Telemetry health shows how many sessions report themselves.
- **Analytics:** Playback reports keep up as your audience grows. Past about a million viewer hours a month, Volt moves them to a dedicated analytics store (ClickHouse) by itself: nothing to set up, no gap in the figures, and the history is rebuilt from the raw reports so every report reads exactly as it did.
- **API:** New playback experience routes under /api/analytics/qoe: overview, series, breakdown, dimensions and definitions, plus POST /api/analytics/qoe/replay to rebuild a period from the raw player reports.
- **API:** New GET /api/analytics/qoe/sessions and GET /api/analytics/qoe/sessions/\{sid\} list the views behind the playback reports and return one session's full timeline; the playback overview now also returns rendering, access, live and ads.
- **API:** New POST /api/public/journey (publishable key) takes an app's journey events, and GET /api/analytics/qoe/journeys and GET /api/analytics/qoe/discovery read the journey to play and discovery reports. Playback report filters take entry_point and session_depth.
- **Channels:** When a programme's rights replace it in some countries, viewers there now see the channel's loop in place of the programme for exactly the replace window, then the programme again, without changing the address they play. Viewers elsewhere keep watching the programme, and recordings are unchanged.
- **Channels:** Rights preview on channel and event pages: pick a country, a rights class and a time (now or any time in the guide) to see what the CDN gives that viewer (normal, replaced or blocked), the rule behind it and until when, play exactly that version before it airs, and see the next 24 hours for every audience the rules name.
- **Channels:** FAST channels can schedule ad breaks between programmes only, with break lengths earned by the content before them, breaks that wait until enough short titles have played, different ad rules by time of day, a cap on ad minutes per clock hour, grid pad sold as ad time, ad-free or "only after" rules per title or slot, and limits on ads per break with one ad per advertiser.
- **Channels:** FAST channels can carry their own brand: idents that play between programmes (in the gaps, or between every programme like a broadcaster) and a brand loop that fills the rest of a gap. The On air tab now shows what is going to air next, to the second, under the preview: programmes, ad breaks, idents and the brand loop, with a countdown to the next item.
- **Delivery:** Live streams now guide players when the delivery path is nearly full: playlists suggest a fair highest bitrate (CMSD), so players step down before the link stalls everyone. A stream delivered over more than one path can steer each viewer's player to the path that works best where they are, measured from the players' own playback reports, without a stall when it moves.
- **Docs:** A new page, How Volt fits together, maps every section of the console and the main ways work flows between them, with a diagram. The Account settings and Audit log guides now show the screens they describe.
- **Docs:** New developer guides: getting started with apps and keys, the play gate and playback tokens, playback reporting and the QoE API, notifications and webhooks, guide import and the EPG API, uploads, and recipes for common integrations. The authentication, lists and player SDK guides follow the same layout, and the player guide now covers DRM and playback reporting. Every code example in them is run against the Volt API before the documentation is published.
- **Documentation:** New guides for Analytics (Audience, Operating and Playback), Plan & limits, Fleet & Usage, Payments, Social accounts, Notifications, Users & Roles, Permissions map and API Keys & Apps, and the Account settings and Audit log guides rewritten in the same form. Each says what the section is for, how to use it step by step, how it connects to the rest of Volt, who can do it, what to do when something goes wrong and the API equivalent. Roles and access now lists the Graphics area and the Graphics Operator role.
- **Documentation:** New guides for EPG, Loops & Fillers, Graphics, Captions and Formats & reframing, each with what it is for, step by step instructions, who can do it, troubleshooting and the API equivalent. The EPG guide now covers repeats, copying a day, the timeline and XMLTV import and export; the Captions guide covers the account's captions engine and each output profile.
- **Documentation:** New guides for every live section: Dashboard, Live Streams, Monitoring, Multiview, Channels, Radio, Events and FAST, each with what it is for, step by step instructions, who can do it, troubleshooting and the API equivalent. The channel status and input failover guides now follow the same layout.
- **Documentation:** New guides for the media sections: Media Library, Recordings, Image Library, Review, Encoding Profiles, Watchfolders, Storage, Workflows, Content Types, Publishing Rules, Ingest Rules, Channel Groups and share links. Each says what the section is for, how to use it step by step, how it connects to the rest of Volt, who can do it, what to do when something goes wrong and the API equivalent.
- **Documentation:** Eight new end-to-end guides show how the sections of Volt work together, each with a diagram: the guide driving recording and publishing, securing a live stream, a 24/7 channel that never goes dark, a live event from start to finish, a FAST channel from the library, a radio station with now playing, captions and graphics on a live stream, and from upload to published.
- **Encoder:** Volt now checks the timing of every live output as it is written: audio and video out of step, a rendition that jumps in time, or a track that stops advancing. Each one shows on the Output gate as a timing fault, in plain words. A packaging channel now cuts its segments on the source's own keyframes, so every quality and the audio share the same boundaries. A stream that is running before anything has been pushed to it now says "awaiting source".
- **EPG:** Viewer apps can now read the programme guide with their app key: what is on now, what is next and what was just on, a channel's day, and a multi-channel grid, from `/api/public/epg`. A FAST channel's guide comes straight from its active lineup, and every schedule change shows in the guide at once.
- **FAST:** A FAST channel now has a channel page like a live channel or an event: **Overview** (laid out like a live channel: the guide strip with what is on now and up next, then a split with the output preview on the left and **On air now** on the right: the asset playing with its poster, position in the asset, metadata, encode (resolution, codecs, bitrates, renditions, formats) and its source file in full; below, what goes to air next, viewers, the log, the playout and its media, graphics on the channel, and every URL: public HLS and DASH, console monitoring, per-viewer stitched sessions and the public guide API), **Build** (run order, week, repeats, ads, brand loop) and **Settings** (name, content type with title, description and genre, logo, guide icon, watermark and Up next graphics). The header has Share, Embed, live graphics control and **Go live** / **Take off air**; Go live activates the run order first when nothing is on air yet. A FAST channel's watermark is drawn by the player over the picture, because its programmes are copied to air without re-encoding: every Volt player, embed and share link shows it, and the play gate gives apps the mark (`meta.brandMark`) to draw themselves.
- **FAST:** FAST channels can use **Loops & Fillers** for filler: under Build > Brand & filler, pick the **slate** (plays in ad breaks nobody sold, and in gaps when there is no loop) and the **gap loop** (fills the gaps between programmes in the slate's place, with or without idents), and optionally play the loop in unsold breaks too. Each slate or loop is rendered once into a broadcast-shaped clip (1920×1080, 25 fps, stereo; a still slate becomes 10 s with silence, a loop up to 2 min, given silence if it has none), prepared for air like any FAST slate, and shared by every channel that uses it. These clips stay out of the Media Library.
- **FAST:** **Audio FAST channels are a clear choice.** New FAST channel now asks "Video channel" or "Audio channel (radio)", and each channel's library and Find media offer only its own media: video for a video channel, audio and songs for an audio channel (the filters can narrow inside that, never leave it). Audio channels get ad breaks too: house spots from audio in the library, and stitched ads where the ad server is told the inventory is audio (`[MEDIA_TYPE]`, `[AD_TYPE]`, `[INVENTORY_TYPE]` tag macros) and an audio creative is preferred.
  **Key metadata in the manifests.** Every programme marker in HLS (`EXT-X-DATERANGE`) and DASH (`EventStream`) now carries the programme's description, genre, series, season, episode, rating, year, language, artist and album, the asset and its length, and what is next (title, start, subtitle, image), for players' now and next displays. Up next cards carry the next programme's key fields too. The Volt player exposes them as `programme.meta` and `nextProgramme`, even with stream graphics off.
- **On-demand ads:** Catch-up without the broadcast's ads: when on-demand ads are on with mid-rolls, a replay or programme recorded from a live channel or event leaves out the ad breaks that were on air (operator breaks and SCTE-35) and plays ads decided for each viewer in their place, with nothing to mark by hand.
- **On-demand ads:** On-demand titles can now carry ad breaks: a pre-roll, mid-rolls at a title's own cue points or every few minutes, and a post-roll, filled by your ad server and then your house spots. Set it up under Admin > On-demand ads for the account or per content type. Viewers who paid play without ads, Apple devices fetch the ads themselves, and every other player gets them stitched in.
- **On-demand ads:** The Volt player now plays on-demand ads the guided way in every browser, on Android and on TVs, not only on Apple devices: it fetches each break's ads when it gets there, shows them in the same picture with the ad count and Learn more, and carries on with the title where it stopped.
- **On-demand ads:** Titles encoded with DASH now get their ads in DASH too: a viewer with stitched ads gets a DASH stream of their own with each ad in its own Period, captions continuing across the breaks and the title's protection kept.
- **On-demand ads:** Your ad server can now decide where the breaks go in on-demand titles (VMAP, as Google Ad Manager's ad rules and FreeWheel do): choose The ad server places them (VMAP) under Where the breaks go. Volt asks for each viewer's schedule, puts each break on the nearest point where the video can switch, plays each break's ads from the ad server, and falls back to your own break settings with house spots when the ad server is slow or down.
- **Player:** Debug mode on every player (right-click > Debug mode, Shift+D, or Cmd/Ctrl+D) now says where a problem is: this device or connection, access rules (region, sign-in), delivery, the stream itself, or the player. It also says whether the problem is worth reporting. A slow connection gets a short note on the picture, so viewers can tell it is their connection and not the stream.
- **Player:** New player options: drm (licence servers by key system, and headers for licence requests) and cmcd (the app's name and version for the reports, or false to turn reporting off). player.sessionId is the one session id the play gate, the Analytics beacons and the CMCD reports share.
- **Player:** The Volt player now reports what each viewer sees, in the CMCD v2 standard. Every playlist and segment request carries CMCD, and the player sends its play states, errors, bitrate changes, a heartbeat every 30 seconds and a sample of response timings to POST /api/public/cmcd. A view starts when play is pressed, so a refused entitlement or a failed licence is still counted, with the step it failed at.
- **Player:** The Volt player marks on-demand ad breaks on the progress bar, yellow for a break still ahead and grey once watched, and the ad's words (Ad 1 of 2, Learn more) follow your player translations.
- **Player SDK:** The player shows on-demand ads with a countdown and Learn more, keeps viewers from seeking inside an ad, and plays a skipped mid-roll before landing where the viewer asked (snapback). Custom skins read state.ad and call commands.adClick().
- **Player SDK:** The Volt player SDK has VoltJourney: a small, fixed set of app events (app open, screens, the title picked and where from, search, paywall and purchase, play) that ties how a viewer reached the play button to what happened after it. Nothing is sent until the viewer's consent allows it, search words are never sent, and the same events can be passed on to Google Analytics 4.
- **Rights:** Blackout and replacement windows from the guide can now differ by audience at the same moment: a viewer in Australia can be replaced from 20:00 to 21:00 while one in New Zealand is replaced from 21:00 to 22:00, each decided per viewer at the CDN on the same play address. Programmes can also exempt a rights class (for example a premium tier) from a window. On a single server the public live address makes the same decision, and outside production you can test it with ?as=AU, &rc= and &at= to see what a viewer elsewhere, or at another time, would get.
- **Social:** Threads: link a Threads profile under Social accounts and publish clips and Image Library pictures to it as posts with their text (up to 500 characters and one hashtag). Views, likes and followers are read back, the publish window shows how many of Threads' 250 daily posts each profile has used, and a sandbox Threads profile lets you try it first.
- **Social accounts:** Instagram: link a Business or Creator account with Instagram Login or through the Facebook Page it is tied to, then publish clips as Reels and Image Library pictures as feed posts. Before you publish, each Instagram account shows how many of its posts for the last 24 hours are used; captions are checked against Instagram's 2,200 characters and 30 hashtags, and views, likes and follower counts are read back.
- **Sources:** **Validating an HLS source shows what metadata it carries.** Besides checking the playlist and the newest segment, the check now reads now playing from the playlist (the title and artist many radio services put in each #EXTINF) and from the ID3 tags at the start of the segment (title, artist, album, artwork, custom text and Apple's timestamp), and reports subtitle tracks, closed captions, EXT-X-DATERANGE and SCTE-35 markers, and whether the stream carries program date-time.
- **VOD:** Media files can be downloaded from the Encode tab, and downloading is now its own permission in Roles & access ("Downloads"). Owners and admins can download; other roles only once an admin grants Downloads. Every download is recorded in the audit log.
- **VOD:** Social formats: Volt follows the faces in a clip and frames a 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5 version of it. The new Formats tab on every asset shows the crop over the picture, a timeline of shots and keyframes and live previews of each format; drag the crop to correct it, choose who a shot follows, approve and render. Shots where two people are too far apart show the whole picture over a blurred fill and are flagged for a look.
- **VOD:** Assets can now carry more than one image. Video keeps its Thumbnail and audio its Cover as the main image, and each asset also offers a Poster, Hero backdrop, Banner, Social share card and Title art for portals, apps and feeds, plus any image types your account adds (Image types… in the asset's image panel). A type without its own image is filled from the main image, cropped to the right shape, so a portal always gets a picture that fits; you can set the focus point that crops keep in frame.
- **VOD:** A new Companion files tab on each asset takes caption and metadata files that belong with it. SRT, WebVTT and TTML captions are checked (timing, overlaps, reading speed, files timed from a timecode) and appear as subtitle tracks in the player for HLS and DASH as soon as they are added, with the language read from the file name. Metadata sidecars (JSON, XML or NFO, CSV) are matched to the asset's fields for you to review and apply.
- **VOD:** You can now delete an asset from the bottom of its Metadata form. As when removing a channel, the dialog shows what goes with it and asks you to type the title. The asset then goes to Storage > Recently removed rather than being destroyed, and an Undo button puts it straight back with its metadata and media. Until the bin empties, you can also restore it from there.
- **VOD:** Genres are now your own list. In Admin > Genres you can add, rename, reorder, retire and merge the genres your stations, channels, events and on-demand assets are tagged with; every Genre and Category field offers your list. Renaming or merging a genre updates everything already tagged with it, a retired genre stays on what has it but is no longer offered, and a genre in use cannot be removed by accident.
- **VOD:** Media > Encoding Queue shows what is being encoded now, what is waiting and in which order, and what finished recently. Open a job to see each output as it is made (every rendition, HLS and DASH, the thumbnails and the sprite sheets), the encoder's log while it runs, and each attempt. Failed encodes can be retried from there, and a thumbnail or sprite step that fails is now reported rather than skipped silently.
- **VOD:** Choose what files are before they upload: the upload panel asks for the content type (or Let the rules decide) and holds files until you pick. Cutting a clip from a recording now offers its content type and fills the title and fields from the guide programme over the marks.
- **VOD:** Collections: group media your own way, nested as deep as you need (a collection per channel, a sub-collection for each week's playlist). Select media in the Media Library list or grid and add it to a collection, browse the library by collection from the new rail with every filter still working, and manage, order and remove collections on the new Collections page. In a FAST channel's run order, the library can add a whole collection in its order, and **Find media** opens the Media Library's full search and filters to add many titles at once.
- **VOD:** Media Library status: every asset shows whether it can be used (Ready, Not checked, QC failed, Building, Build failed, Will not play, No media), with a Status filter and a Problems shortcut. A failed build says why on the asset and offers Build again, or Build accepting the gaps when the recording has holes. Only Ready assets are offered to FAST channels, planned onto air, or accepted as a blackout replacement.
- **VOD:** **Trailer and Music video content types.** Two new standard content types for on-demand assets. **Trailer / promo** (schema.org VideoObject): title, what it is a trailer for, kind (trailer, teaser, promo, featurette, clip), description, release date, genre, rating, language and image. **Music video** (schema.org MusicVideoObject): title, artist, album, director, genre, label, ISRC, release date, explicit and image, matching the Song type so a music channel reads both the same way. Every account gets them automatically, and the library's metadata completeness covers them.
- **VOD:** Uploads are read by their file names: S01E03 makes an episode with its series, season and number, "Official Trailer" makes a trailer, and the title is tidied (`7th-voyage-of-sinbad_1958_trailer_1080p.webm` becomes "7th Voyage Of Sinbad"). Only what is clear is applied, nothing you typed or chose is replaced, and **Read from file name** on an asset or a selection shows each reading and why. A trailer's **Trailer for** now links to the film itself: Volt links it when one record matches clearly and suggests the closest ones when it does not. Your own file-name rules live in Ingest Rules, and the API returns linked records with `?include=linkedContent`.

## Improved

- **Account:** The Image Library tidies itself. Folders that retention or an asset's removal leaves empty are removed, and folder counts now match what the grid shows. Song artwork has its own Cover type and filter. Duplicates… finds images stored more than once, keeps one and points everything that used a copy at it. Choosing an image for an asset now opens on that asset's own frames, uploads and stills first, and a video is no longer offered song covers.
- **Analytics:** The Analytics page now says what it shows (who watched, for how long and where, by day) instead of how its figures are stored.
- **Analytics:** Live playback reports stay exact when Volt runs on several servers: each viewer session is now counted by one server, and yesterday is rebuilt from the raw player reports every night.
- **Console:** The console now works on a phone or tablet: under 900 px wide the menu opens as a drawer from the button at the top left, and pages no longer scroll sideways; wide tables scroll inside their card and the pipeline status wraps into rows.
- **Delivery:** The CDN now lets players read its own request id (x-amz-cf-id) and request timings, so a player's report can be matched to the CDN's log line. CMCD on requests never changes what is cached: viewers still share one copy of each segment.
- **EPG:** A programme's slate or loop replacement is now chosen from Loops & Fillers, the same place as a stream's fallback, so a loop uploaded there can be picked for a slot. Slots saved earlier with an Image Library image keep working and offer a one-step copy into Loops & Fillers.
- **Formats & reframing:** Instagram Reels get a clip's 9:16 version whenever one is already made and approved, even with Send each platform its own format off.
- **On-demand ads:** Every pause, resume, mute and unmute during an ad now reaches your ad server, not just the first of each.
- **Player SDK:** The Volt player now reports ad breaks (abs and abe) and the HTTP status of a refused request, so ads and playback tokens refused mid-view show in the playback reports.
- **Rights preview:** When the replacement loop is not ready, the rights preview now says why: built from the stream's slate or loop video and not ready yet, no slate or loop set, or input failover off.
- **VOD:** Images Volt saves for an asset are now named after the asset, like sintel-1080p-21fe57ffc0.jpg, in a folder named the same way and dated by the programme, the recording or the day it was added (no more 1970-01-01, and no .mkv in folder names). Existing images are renamed in the background, everything that uses them keeps working, and renaming an asset renames its images.
- **VOD:** The asset panel in the Media Library can now be made wider: drag its left edge, or use the width button next to Close to switch between normal, wide and full width. Your choice is remembered next time you open an asset.
- **VOD:** In the Media Library you can now see which asset you are working on: its card is ringed and labelled Open, stays marked Last opened after you close it, and keyboard focus returns to it. A card whose image, title or availability just changed pulses briefly and shows Updated. The list view marks the open row the same way.
- **VOD:** The Image Library no longer fills up with sprite sheets. The previews behind scrub bars, recording and clip timelines are now kept in their own store, out of sight, and sheets already in the library are moved there automatically.
- **Watch folders:** Programmes dropped into a watch folder now go through your ingest rules, as uploads do: naming, duplicate check, metadata and webhooks. Scan reports how many went to the Media Library for encoding, how many were added to Loops & Fillers and how many the ingest rules held.
- **Watchfolders:** On AWS, a watchfolder in a storage bucket now takes a file in as soon as it finishes uploading, usually within seconds, instead of waiting for the next check of the folder. Put the folder under watch/ in Volt's own bucket; every bucket folder is still looked through once an hour in case a file was missed.
- **Workflows:** Hovering over a stage on the Workflows map now shows only what the stage does, and the add-on contract points at the Workflows guide.

## Fixed

- **Account:** Account settings now warns when a primary colour is a mid-tone that neither white nor dark text reads well on. The warning was described in the guide but could never appear.
- **Account:** Moving or renaming an image in the Image Library now updates every place that uses it, including an asset's candidate frames and chosen song covers, so nothing loses its picture. Links left behind by earlier moves are repaired automatically.
- **Account:** Moving or renaming a video in the Image Library (an animated bug) now moves its video, its thumbnail and its original file together, so it keeps playing and its card shows a picture. Videos split by an earlier move are put back together automatically.
- **API:** GET /api/jobs/\{id\} and the jobs list now include the encode job of an uploaded or watch folder file, for its own account.
- **Channels:** Example names in the New channel and New station forms, the Icecast address example and the Radio empty state no longer name real broadcasters or stations.
- **Encoder:** When a source drops and comes back, the stream now carries on from where it was instead of jumping back in time, so playback no longer breaks after a failover. A timing warning no longer shows as "encoder failed". Admins now get an inbox message (and an email and push where those are set up) when a stream's encoder stops unexpectedly, its source cannot be read, it has been on the slate for over a minute, or its output timing goes wrong. Each stream sends at most one alert per problem every half hour.
- **EPG:** Closing the programme form while it shows an overlap warning no longer hides the new programme: the guide reloads and lists it straight away.
- **EPG:** Escape now closes the Copy or repeat day dialog and the library image picker.
- **Events:** New event now starts on a workflow your account has a group for, and the Workflow cards say when a workflow has no group, with a note under them, instead of only after Create event fails. New channel also settles on the right type when it opens before your groups have loaded.
- **FAST:** FAST now follows the TV channels level of each role: View to see FAST channels, run orders and previews, Operate to put a channel on or off air and activate a run order, Manage to create channels and edit run orders, ads and ad servers. A role without TV channels can no longer read or change FAST channels through the API. Ad breaks follow their stream: an event's breaks need Events and a station's need Radio stations.
- **FAST:** On a run order that rounds programmes up to the half hour or hour, What this run order does to a week now counts each programme as the guide places it, so one pass, passes this week and when the next pass starts match the Week tab.
- **FAST:** Stitched ads can now be set up entirely in the console: choosing Stitched ads with no ad server opens the ad server form, and Add and use saves both. A FAST channel can be made Public only once a run order is active, so viewers are never given an empty channel.
- **FAST:** The FAST time zone list now has UTC, first, so a channel on UTC shows UTC instead of the first zone in the list. An empty run order no longer reports thousands of one-minute passes a week; it asks you to add titles or series.
- **FAST:** FAST channels now play when Volt runs with the Docker encoder launcher (run-on-mac.sh): the playout runs on the host, as it does with the local launcher, instead of never starting and leaving the channel at "Starting". The FAST channel page has a **Start** button (and Restart the playout) like a live channel, and a **QC** drawer that checks the run order, guide, media, playout, output manifest, what is on air, the public URL, watermark and ads. Deleting a media asset no longer fails with "operator does not exist: bigint = uuid" when something linked to it (review notes, QC runs, ad rows) is keyed by a number rather than a uuid.
- **FAST:** **FAST channels play one ladder.** A FAST channel now has a ladder (picked when it is made, the account default unless another is chosen, changeable on On air), and every title, house spot, slate and ident on it is packaged to exactly that ladder in the background. Before, the channel took its renditions from its first title, so a smaller source, or a film wider than 16:9 whose height dropped the 1080p rendition, put 720p pictures under the 1080p rendition and players stalled at the join. A title whose own encode already matches is used as it is; anything else gets a copy made for the ladder beside its own encode (letterboxed to 16:9, every rendition, the channel's frame rate), recorded against the title so other channels on the same ladder reuse it.
  **The library offers what can fill the channel.** A channel's library lists sources at or above its top rendition, by picture size and bitrate (a scope film counts by its width), and says how many smaller titles it is leaving out, with a way to show them anyway. On air shows the ladder, how many titles are packaged to it and the progress of the rest.
  **Ladder, slate and loop in a FAST channel's Settings.** Settings now has the channel's ladder beside its slate and gap loop (which moved there from Build > Brand). Pick a ladder or make one, and set its renditions (height and bitrate, or a standard HD, HD lite or UHD set), picture sizes, frame rate, encoder, formats and segment length right there. It is the same editor Encoding profiles now uses, and it says what else shares the ladder before you change it; channels on it re-package what they air on their own.
  **Ingest straight into the channel's renditions.** A ladder can now make fixed sizes: every rendition at its 16:9 size, letterboxed where the picture's shape differs, none dropped. Make such a ladder the account default (or a watch folder's) and uploads are encoded to exactly the renditions a FAST channel on that ladder plays, so they go to air without a second encode.
- **Graphics:** Save and assign on a new graphics setup now opens the Assign form. Before, it saved the setup and stopped there.
- **Media Library:** Publish and Remove access now work on files that were uploaded or dropped into a watch folder. Before, both answered Asset not found for any asset that did not come from a channel or event.
- **Monitoring:** Multiview now plays the output viewers are actually given, at the live edge and with sound in step with the picture. Starting and stopping a tile no longer jumps back several minutes. Each tile shows source and output flags (NO SOURCE, failover, filler, dropped outputs, timing), and the wall pages itself to what fits your screen. "Viewer location" plays every tile as a viewer in a chosen country sees it, with the rights decision and its reason beside the picture.
- **On-demand ads:** Stitched HLS of a protected title no longer marks its clear ads as encrypted: the key is closed before each break and stated again where the title resumes. Guided playlists now mark pre- and post-rolls with the CUE attribute players read, and a mid-roll plays once.
- **Permissions map:** The audit log row points at Admin > Audit log, where the audit log now lives.
- **Platform:** The API no longer stops when the database drops an idle connection (a restart or failover): the connection is replaced and requests carry on.
- **Publishing:** Approving a clip that was waiting for review now publishes it when its publishing rule has Auto-publish, and a full-event replay (Automatic VOD on end or Publish full VOD) is published under its rule as soon as it is made. Uploaded and watch folder files are still published by hand.
- **Radio:** The example address under an Icecast source, and the example name for a new channel group, no longer name a real station or customer.
- **Share links:** The Share window no longer says a preview ignores geo rules. A share link follows the countries your account allows, and the window now says so.
- **Social accounts:** Social accounts is in the menu only for people who can open it: it needs Account settings: View and Media library & publishing: View, so a role with settings alone no longer gets a page that cannot load.
- **Users & Roles:** The Public users tab shows how many public users the account has, instead of always 0.
- **VOD:** Saving an item's metadata, a skip marker or a QC re-run now confirms that it worked, or says why it did not. When song details are applied with a cover, the cover is copied into the Image Library first and the Artwork field is filled with it, including when the item is made a Song at the same time.
- **VOD:** Choosing an image for an asset now shows on its card in the Media Library straight away, and as the poster in its player, without refreshing. Before, the grid could keep showing the previous picture even after a refresh. Cards also update by themselves when Volt picks an image in the background or an asset changes, while the library is open.
- **VOD:** Choosing a new image for an asset now replaces the old one straight away, everywhere it is shown. Before, a new frame could keep showing the old picture, and an image set in the Metadata form was ignored if someone had chosen the image by hand earlier. The Metadata form now also shows clearly when it has unsaved changes, and asks before you switch tab, open another asset or close it with changes unsaved.
- **VOD:** A clip whose build failed no longer sits in the library looking normal: the failure is written to the asset. Automatic VOD from the guide and scheduled clips now accept up to 60 s of missing recording (QC flags the gap) instead of failing outright, and a clip that skips a gap no longer fails at the last step.
- **VOD:** QC no longer passes an asset it could not measure. An asset with no media, or media that will not open or decode, now fails QC with the reason, and QC reads the HLS manifest a viewer plays rather than an MP4 that a streaming clip never has. The QC panel lists every check: what it found, that it was clean, or why it could not run. A picture with no audio track is reported.
- **VOD:** An upload whose content type has a required field left empty (a bug's Advertiser, for example) is now encoded straight away, with its renditions, thumbnails and preview sprites, instead of sitting at "No media" until the field is filled. Uploaded H.264 / AAC MP4 and MOV files also get an MP4 beside HLS and DASH, and the asset page shows the source file's full technical details: container, size, duration, overall and per-stream bitrates, codec with profile and level, resolution, aspect, frame rate, scan, bit depth, colour, and every audio and subtitle track.
- **Watch folders:** A loop dropped into a watch folder now arrives in Loops & Fillers ready to use as a channel or event fallback, the same as an uploaded loop. Before, it was listed but could not be used.
- **Watch folders:** A programme or loop dropped into a watch folder is added once. Before, the same file could be added again, and encoded again, every ten minutes.
- **Watchfolders:** Files that a watchfolder in a storage bucket accepted were never added to the library and stayed Waiting. They are now added like files from any other watchfolder, and the downloaded copy is removed once the library has it.

## Changed

- **Console:** The sign-in form is empty in the shipped console; the demo login is filled in only when running the console in development.
- **Social accounts:** Social accounts, Notifications and Fleet & Usage no longer show account users the server's setting names or cloud machine names. A channel or platform that is not set up says your Volt provider sets it up, and Fleet & Usage describes each server as shared, for one stream or on-premises. Whoever runs the Volt deployment still sees the full detail.
- **Social accounts:** Facebook publishing now uses version 25.0 of Meta's Graph API (version 21.0 stops working in January 2027). Nothing changes in how you publish.
- **Social publishing:** X is now off by default, because X charges for every post and every read. Your Volt provider can switch it on for your account, or you can add your own X developer app under Social accounts so X bills you directly. X accounts you already linked are kept and shown as off until X is on again.
- **VOD:** The Media Library now filters by asset type (Video, Audio, Song) and by source (Channel, Event, Radio, Upload, or No source for items whose channel or event is gone). Content type moves under More filters, and how an item was made is now labelled "Created as". The list's Source column says which kind of source each item came from.
- **VOD:** **An asset's image is in its metadata.** The content type's main image field (Image, Thumbnail, Cover or Artwork) now shows the image the asset already uses, chosen frame or default, instead of an empty box, and follows it when a better image is picked. An image someone sets in that field still wins and is never overwritten. Existing assets are filled in once on upgrade.
- **VOD:** Content can be created and saved with required metadata fields still empty, through the console or the API, and filled in along the way. Publishing checks them instead: it is refused until every required field is filled, and says which ones are missing.
