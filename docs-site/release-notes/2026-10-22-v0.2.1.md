Changes to FAST and Radio.

<!-- truncate -->

## Fixed

- **FAST:** Taking a FAST channel on or off air now counts towards its on-air hours and as-run reports, the same as any other channel. Before, those hours were missing from the totals.
- **Radio:** The Now playing column in the station list now shows the same line as the station page. A station on an HLS source, which cannot send titles, shows a dash instead of Untitled break, and Untitled break is kept for an Icecast station between two titles.
