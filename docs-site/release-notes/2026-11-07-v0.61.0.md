Changes to Mirror and Archive.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **Mirror and Archive:** A new Nightly option on each storage target copies, once a day, anything the rule covers that the target does not hold yet, including items that came in before the rule existed. Nightly copies wait for the off-peak window.
- **Mirror and Archive:** Copy rules can now copy an item's sidecars beside its original: a metadata file, the caption files as they were delivered, other files that came with it, and its poster, each named after the original. Tick Sidecars under Copy for a storage target in the Archive copies field.
