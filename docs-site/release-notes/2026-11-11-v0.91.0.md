Changes to API, Guides and Media Library.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **API:** New on-demand routes: PUT /api/vod/\{id\}/availability (Listed from, Unlisted), POST /api/vod/\{id\}/archive and /unarchive, and POST /api/vod/\{id\}/evaluate (who can watch). GET /api/vod and GET /api/vod/\{id\} carry the public status, and the list filters on public_status and expires_within_days.
- **Media Library:** The Availability tab now shows what viewers get: the public status and why, a timeline of the title's life with today marked, Listed from and Unlisted, Archive and Restore, and Who can watch, which tests a country, a kind of viewer and a date against the real play decision.
- **Media Library:** The library has a Public status column and filter, including Expires in 7 days for titles about to stop being available.

## Fixed

- **Guides:** The links from the FAST from the library workflow to the FAST guide's run order and ads sections open those sections again.
