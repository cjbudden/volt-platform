Changes to FAST and On-demand ads.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **FAST:** FAST channels report who saw what, and replace for DASH too: Build > Brand & filler > Rights delivery lists every scheduled programme with the viewers who saw it, saw the geo replacement, or were refused, in total and per country, for today, the last 7 or the last 31 days (also GET /api/fast/channels/\{id\}/rights-report). Viewers are counted in memory as they play and written once a minute without slowing playback or locking the database. DASH viewers outside a title's rights now get the geo replacement as HLS viewers do.

## Improved

- **On-demand ads:** Stitched ads now play where play tokens are enforced at the CDN. A viewer's stream with ads carries that viewer's own tokens for the title and for each ad, so a copied address does not play in another country, and the stream stops when the title is withdrawn.
