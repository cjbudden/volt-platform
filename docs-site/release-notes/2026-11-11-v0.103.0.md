Changes to FAST and On-demand ads.

<!-- truncate -->

## Improved

- **FAST:** Ads and house spots stitched into a viewer's FAST breaks now play where play tokens are enforced: they carry the channel's shared token, and a withdrawn ad stops within about a minute.
- **On-demand ads:** Guided ads now play where play tokens are enforced at the CDN: the shared guided playlist names the title's segments through a shared token that outlasts the title, so a viewer who starts just before the token changes can still watch to the end. A withdrawn title's segments stop within about a minute.
