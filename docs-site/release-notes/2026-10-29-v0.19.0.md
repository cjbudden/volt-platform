Changes to Documentation and Encoding Queue.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **Documentation:** New guides for Collections, the Encoding Queue, Genres and Single sign-on: how to group and order library media, follow and retry encodes, keep the genre list tidy, and let your team sign in with your own identity provider.

## Fixed

- **Encoding Queue:** An encode's page no longer shows customer accounts the name of the machine running it, and its packages read Stored rather than naming a storage bucket.
