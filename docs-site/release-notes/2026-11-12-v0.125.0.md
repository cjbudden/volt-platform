## New

- **Media Library:** A Repackage pending filter lists the titles whose files do not yet carry what their publishing policy asks for (DRM on or off).

## Fixed

- **Media Library:** QC and frame stills of a DRM-protected title now read its original, so they no longer report a working title as unplayable; with no original on hand QC skips those checks and says why.
