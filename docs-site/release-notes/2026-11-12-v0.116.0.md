Changes to API and Player.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **Player:** When a viewer's account starts more plays than a title's Concurrent streams allows, the Volt player on the oldest device now stops, says why and offers Play here to move the play back to it. Closing or hiding a player's page frees its stream at once.

## Improved

- **API:** A play stopped by Concurrent streams can no longer carry on with its old addresses: within about a minute the CDN refuses its on-demand manifests and segments with 403 Playing on too many devices, while the viewer's other plays are untouched.
