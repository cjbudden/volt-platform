## New

- **Billing:** Where your Volt provider bills through Volt, your plan fee, server time and any agreed charges or credits are gathered into one invoice per calendar month, issued on the 1st. Read this month so far and your invoices, with their lines, through the API (GET /api/billing), and download any invoice as CSV.
