Changes to API and Collections.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **API:** GET /api/collections/\{id\}/access now gives each app a ruledOut count: how many of the collection's titles its rights keep out of that app, with a few of their titles.
- **Collections:** Set who can watch a collection from its page: choose the apps and territories it applies to, then region by region whether viewers need nothing, to sign in, or a package, with the package's products and an app's own store prices. A sub-collection shows what it inherits, and apps the rights of every title keep out are greyed with the reason.
- **Collections:** View as on a collection shows what a viewer gets for each title in an app and a country (not signed in, signed in or holding a package): plays, sign in, locked with the packages and prices that unlock it, or blocked by rights, and which collections decided; below it, a matrix of your apps by regions.
