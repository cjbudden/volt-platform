Changes to Media Library and Recordings.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **Media Library:** The video preview shows the timecode and frame number, and while paused the arrow keys step one frame at a time, showing the exact frame.
- **Recordings:** Step through a recording frame by frame: while paused the arrow keys move one frame and show that exact frame with its timecode, In and Out are set on it, and small pictures under the marks show the clip's exact first and last frames.
