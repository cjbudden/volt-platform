## Changed

- **Storage:** Exact-frame stills are now tracked by where they are kept (bucket and folder) and follow the retention ladder's Delete rung: they go when their recording is removed, when their asset is deleted or re-encoded, and 7 days after they were last made. Each folder of stills is listed on the Storage page.
