Changes to Media Library and Play gate.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **Media Library:** Every title now has a public status worked out by Volt, with the reason and when it changes next: draft, in review, processing, scheduled, available, restricted by its rights, expired or archived. It follows publishing, withdrawing and the end of a window within a minute, and is ready for the Availability tab coming next.

## Changed

- **Play gate:** Only published titles that are available now are public. The play gate and the access check answer a draft, a title in review, still processing, outside its rights, expired or archived exactly as an id that does not exist (404), so nothing about unpublished content can be learned from them. A title announced ahead of its release answers 403 with mode scheduled and when it opens.
