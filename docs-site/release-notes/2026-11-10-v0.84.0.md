Changes to Collections, FAST and Play gate.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **Collections:** A collection can now be sold as a package, or limited by app and region: choose the apps and countries it applies to and, region by region, whether its titles are free, need viewers to sign in, or need a package bought, priced per app. The strictest collection a title is in wins in each app and country, buying any of the packages that lock it unlocks it, a sub-collection can only tighten its parent's access, and a title whose rights rule out the collection's access is refused with the reason. Set through the API for now, with View as to check what a viewer gets.
- **FAST:** FAST channels apply each title's rights where viewers are: while a channel airs, a viewer outside a title's territories, FAST rights or rights window (or the geo of its publishing plan) sees the channel's geo replacement for that programme, then the channel again. Choose the replacement, a loop or slate from Loops & Fillers, under Build > Brand & filler, where the programmes restricted in the next two days are listed. Settings has a new Where it can be watched for the whole channel. The FAST guide is rewritten to cover every tab and option, with examples of what each does on air.

## Improved

- **Play gate:** A title locked by a collection package answers 402 with the packages that unlock it and the products the viewer's app sells, and a members-only collection answers 403 to sign in.
