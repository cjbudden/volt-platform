Changes to Collections and Loops & Fillers.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **Collections:** Each app chooses how its collection rows treat a title a package locks: show it locked, with the package that unlocks it and what viewers can buy, or leave it out. Set it as Titles a package locks on the app in API Keys & Apps; rows follow the change at once.
- **Loops & Fillers:** Each QC finding on a slate or loop now shows stills from the preview at that moment (for a jump where a loop restarts, its last frame beside its first), plays that moment with one click, and can be accepted on its own, with a note. Accepted findings stay accepted when QC runs again, and once every error is accepted the item can be approved without a note.

## Improved

- **Collections:** A rule collection's row in your app leaves out a title another collection sells as a package in that app and country, as View as already showed; a title added by hand stays, listed as locked.

## Fixed

- **Collections:** A collection row in your app left out a title a collection package locks, instead of listing it as premium. Removing a collection's access could also leave a title locked until the next background pass; it now changes at once.
- **Loops & Fillers:** Who approved, rejected or archived a slate or loop is shown by name or email, not as an id.

## Changed

- **Loops & Fillers:** The actions on each row of the list are now icons, named when you hover: details (or review, for one awaiting approval), archive or make available, and delete.
