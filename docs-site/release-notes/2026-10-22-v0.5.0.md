<!-- truncate -->

## Improved

- **Media Library:** The Encode tab now shows what each asset actually carries, read from its own files: the video profile and level of every rendition, each audio track with its codec, channels, sample rate and language, and, for a package-only stream, the codec and audio the contributor sent. File encodes also say which of the source's audio tracks was used, that subtitle tracks in the file are not carried yet, and that the result is not DRM protected.
