Changes to Channels.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **Channels:** Ad breaks on live channels and events now carry SCTE-35 in the delivered streams: an SCTE-35 cue on the break in HLS, cue out and cue in on the segments it covers, and an SCTE-35 event in DASH. Downstream ad insertion and distribution partners see every break you start, schedule or send through the API.
