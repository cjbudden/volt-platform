Changes to API, Channel Groups and Encoding Profiles.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **API:** POST /api/channels/\{id\}/group and POST /api/events/\{id\}/group move a stream to another group; PATCH /api/encode-profiles/\{id\} now checks its values and answers a taken name, a duplicate height in a ladder or an out-of-date version with a clear 409.
- **Channel Groups:** Move channels, radio stations, FAST channels and events to another group of the same workflow with Move streams on Channel Groups (also offered when a group in use cannot be removed). Volt checks the target group's share and running at once, and says when a running stream picks up the new group's output profile at its next start.
- **Encoding Profiles:** Edit a rendition's name, height and bitrate in place; every ladder containing it follows, and FAST channels on those ladders re-package on their own.

## Improved

- **Channel Groups:** View its streams on a group opens the Live Streams list filtered to that group, and the list has a group picker.

## Fixed

- **Encoding Profiles:** Delete is no longer offered on a rendition a ladder uses, and users who can only view the page no longer see buttons that would be refused.
