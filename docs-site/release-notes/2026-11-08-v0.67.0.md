Changes to Hybrid Sites.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **Hybrid Sites:** On-prem watchfolders can now be set to Encode at site: video and audio are registered in the Media Library and encoded by your site's servers, only the finished streams are uploaded, and the original stays on your storage. Images, captions, slates and loops in the same folder are still uploaded.
