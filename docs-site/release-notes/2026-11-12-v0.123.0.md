Changes to Media Library and Publishing Rules.

<!-- truncate -->

## New

- **Publishing Rules:** Confirming a DRM change on published titles now packages them again on their own, in the background and in batches: each title keeps playing with its current protection until its new files are complete, then switches. Before you save and the confirmation say about how long the jobs take.

## Improved

- **Media Library:** The Encode tab shows Repackage queued or Repackaging while a title is being packaged again after a DRM change.
