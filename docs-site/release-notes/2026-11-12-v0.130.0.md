Changes to Recordings.

<!-- truncate -->

## Improved

- **Recordings:** Recordings show the logo of the channel or radio station they belong to, in the Recordings list, on a source's page and on each recording. Open channel, Open station or Open event goes straight to what the recording belongs to.

## Fixed

- **Recordings:** A radio station whose settings were not fully saved yet could show its recordings as a channel's.
