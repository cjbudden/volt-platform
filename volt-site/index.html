Two signature workflows

Every live hour becomes a library. Every library becomes a channel.

The current runs both ways. Volt records what goes to air, cuts it into clips and programmes, and schedules the library back out as live and FAST channels.

Live → VOD

Recording starts when the channel goes on air. Nothing to remember, nothing to export.

  1. Every channel and event is recorded as it airs, with its EPG and as-run log.
  2. Trim, clip and publish from the timeline while the stream is still live.
  3. Programmes land in the media library with captions, images and metadata.

VOD → Live

Turn your archive into a channel. Schedule it like a broadcaster, run it like a playlist.

  1. Build a FAST channel or a loop from library assets and slates.
  2. Schedule by daypart, repeat rules and recurrence, with conflicts flagged.
  3. Publish an XMLTV guide and a stable play URL your apps never have to change.

Pricing

Pay for server time and data. That is it.

A plan sets how many channels you can have and how many run at once. Channels are a flat monthly price, events are billed by the minute of server time, and bandwidth is per GB delivered.

Prices in US dollars, excluding tax. Event server time has a short minimum per event to cover spin-up and testing. Your bill shows server minutes and GB delivered for every channel and event.
